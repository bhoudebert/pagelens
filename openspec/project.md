# Project: Pagelens

## Purpose

Provide evidence-grounded, local multimodal retrieval across user-owned PDF collections.

## Conventions

- Specifications use RFC 2119 terms (`MUST`, `SHOULD`, `MAY`).
- Capabilities live under `openspec/specs/<capability>/spec.md`.
- Proposed behavioral changes live under `openspec/changes/<change-id>/` until accepted.
- Public behavior follows Semantic Versioning and is recorded in `CHANGELOG.md`.
- Source modules follow ports-and-adapters boundaries; model and storage providers remain
  replaceable.
